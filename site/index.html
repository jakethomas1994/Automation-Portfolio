02About

Often the lead tester on a project. Frequently a solo tester trusted to ship features alone.

I'm a Senior Test Engineer specialising in mobile and product QA. My work spans hands-on exploratory testing, network-level debugging with Charles Proxy, and building automated UI suites that run in CI/CD.

Before the BBC I tested at Atom bank — the UK's first app-only bank — building the SIT suites and testing biometric authentication. I care about the details users feel — playback that just works, ratings that climb, and releases that land cleanly during the biggest live moments of the year.
